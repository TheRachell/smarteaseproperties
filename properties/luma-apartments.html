About the project

Luma Apartments is a premium serviced apartment development by SmartEase Properties Limited, located at 12 Bailey Street, Abule Ijesha, Yaba, Lagos, one of Lagos's fastest-growing residential and commercial districts.

Abule Ijesha sits as a high-yield gateway between the Lagos Mainland and Island markets, right next to UNILAG and YabaTech, with quick connectivity to the Third Mainland Bridge. This combination keeps demand from students, corporate tenants and shortlet guests consistently strong.

The SmartEase Properties thesis

At Luma Apartments, we're offering investors direct ownership of a residential real estate asset within an established urban housing market. The investment case is built on the property's continuing residential utility, multiple income opportunities and flexibility of exit, not on assumptions of extraordinary price appreciation.

Our approach evaluates every opportunity against four fundamentals: total acquisition cost, realistic income potential, downside protection and potential exit value, built to support attractive economics without depending on aggressive rental assumptions or speculative future price growth.

In simple terms: own the asset, generate income, build equity, and retain the flexibility to hold, refinance or sell when the economics are favourable.
